OpenWOP RFCs

91 RFCs governing additive evolution of the v1 protocol. Status legend: Draft (open comment window) → Active (accepted; impl follows) → Accepted (in-tree reference impl + conformance scenarios) → Withdrawn / Superseded.

v1.1 is the stable wire contract. RFCs at Draft or Active target v1.2 (additive) unless the RFC is explicitly labelled Safety fix per COMPATIBILITY.md §3. No RFC may land a breaking change inside v1.x.
