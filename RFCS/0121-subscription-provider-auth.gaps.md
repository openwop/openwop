| G1 | Motivation / Unresolved Q1 | No confirmed provider terms-of-service permits API-shaped third-party automation under a reused consumer-subscription login, for any vendor. | Spec Architect | Provider-specific legal/ToS review, or discovery of a provider-sanctioned integration path.
