# RFC 0090 — Risk Register

Companion to `0090-shadow-run-contract.md`. Likelihood × Impact (H/M/L). Critical/High risks require a named mitigation owner + target resolution.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | A host implements `shadow.diverged` / `shadow.compared` naively and includes **raw output values** (which may be PII / financial data) in the payload or event log. | M | H | **High** | Normative MUST-NOT (digests + metrics only); new `SECURITY/invariants.yaml` row; enforcing conformance scenario `shadow-comparison-content-free`; a worked redaction example in `shadow-run.md`. | Security Architect | Open |
| R2 | A `replay`-mode baseline references **another tenant's** run, leaking cross-tenant outputs into a comparison. | L | H | **Med** | Normative cross-tenant refusal (`forbidden_tenant`, CTI-1); conformance scenario `shadow-cross-tenant-baseline-refused`. | Security Architect | Open |
| R3 | The baseline leg performs **production side-effects** (re-posts an invoice, re-sends an email) because the host runs it as an ordinary run. | M | H | **High** | Normative observe-only requirement; baseline reuses replay idempotency (`replay.md §Determinism`); conformance scenario `shadow-baseline-no-side-effects`. | Spec Architect | Open |
| R4 | Scope creep — "shadow comparison" balloons into a full **eval harness** (golden sets, scoring rubrics, regression gates). | M | M | Med | RFC scoped to per-run baseline comparison only; eval-set authoring (EV-1/EV-2) is explicitly out of scope and a separate track; Open spec gaps table records the boundary. | Spec Architect | Open |
| R5 | Comparison **non-determinism** — two hosts (or two reads) disagree on whether a value diverged because canonicalization differs. | M | M | Med | Pin the digest canonicalization to the replay cache-key recipe (G5); determinism scenario `shadow-comparison-deterministic`. | Conformance Architect | Open |
| R6 | Additive surface adopted by hosts that stay on conformance suite 1.0 → **INTEROP-MATRIX drift** (capability advertised, scenarios not run). | M | L | Low | Gate every scenario on `capabilities.shadow.supported`; RFC §Conformance reminder; INTEROP-MATRIX column added when the first reference host implements. | Conformance Architect | Open |
| R7 | RFC freezes shapes the **demo pilot then contradicts** (the "Shadow & Prove" stage learns the real shape only at build time). | M | M | Med | Land at `Draft` (shapes may shift); hold `Active` until the pilot exercises §B/§D/§E; steward ruling: freeze only what the pilot has exercised. | Compatibility Architect | Open |
