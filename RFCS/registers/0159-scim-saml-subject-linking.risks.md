# RFC 0159 — Risk register

Risks identified while authoring RFC 0159 (SCIM ⟷ SAML subject linking). Scored Likelihood × Impact (H/M/L). Critical/High rows carry a named mitigation owner.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A host implements the link on **email/`userName`** (mutable/PII) → account-takeover join: a caller who sets their IdP email to a victim's inherits the victim's SAML subject | M | H | **High** | §A.2 forbids it as a normative MUST NOT; the `auth-subject-link.test.ts` negative leg asserts an email "link" yields no cross-lane pass; UQ2 proposes a discovery-declared key class to make the claim witnessable | Security Architect | Open (mitigated by §A.2 + negative scenario) |
| R2 | Opt-in flag means a naive combined host keeps the leaver bypass and is still "conforming" → the security gap persists in the field under a conforming banner | M | M | Med | Deliberate additive-first sequencing (UQ1); INTEROP-MATRIX `subjectLinking` column makes non-adoption visible; follow-up breaking RFC can make it mandatory | Compatibility Architect | Open (accepted trade-off) |
| R3 | A host merges the two durable subjects instead of linking → rewrites a subject key stamped on historical runs → breaks `:fork` replay + RFC 0048 §D owner-echo determinism | L | H | Med | §A + Implementation notes mandate link-not-merge and explicitly forbid touching `userIdFor`; Alternatives §2 records why merge is rejected | Spec Architect | Open (mitigated by normative link-not-merge) |
| R4 | §A.2/§A.4 negative-existence properties can't be fully witnessed (a suite can't prove a host never joins on a mutable key for an unprobed pair) → an unsafe host passes the suite | M | M | Med | Falsifiability table records the residual; UQ2's declared key-class converts it to a witnessable advertisement; until then it is a documented claims-check, not a silent gap | Conformance Architect | Mitigated (RFC 0163 §A, 2026-09-01 — declared class + schema conditional; residual narrowed to a host that lies in discovery and is never probed on the lying pair, RFC 0163 UQ5) |
| R5 | Cross-tenant link (bug or misconfig) links subjects across tenants → cross-tenant escalation | L | H | Med | §A.1 scopes the link to a single tenant as a MUST; composes with RFC 0048 §D / RFC 0011 tenant-isolation invariants; a conformance follow-up could add a cross-tenant negative | Security Architect | Mitigated (same-tenant MUST; the cross-IdP axis closed by RFC 0163 §B, invariant `subject-link-same-trust-root`, 2026-09-01) |
