# RFC 0121 — Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A host (or an implementer following this RFC) advertises/implements `subscription` mode using the direct-API-under-borrowed-token shape (§C shape 1) for a provider whose consumer ToS actually forbids it, exposing operators and end users to account suspension or legal exposure. | M | H | **Critical** | Filed `Draft (Parked)` with Unresolved question 1 as an explicit un-parking tripwire; Acceptance criteria require a documented per-provider ToS resolution before `Active`. No reference-host or ADR work is authorized until this clears. | Security Architect | Open — gating condition, not yet resolved |
| R2 | A host advertises `subscription` support and permits binding the resulting credential at tenant/workspace scope, silently sharing one individual's personal, single-seat subscription across a multi-user tenant — both a ToS violation and an unaudited access-sharing surface. | M | H | High | New normative MUST (§B clause 8) + `subscription-credential-user-scope-only` SECURITY invariant (protocol-tier, `high` severity) + a dedicated conformance scenario asserting tenant-scope binding is rejected with `credential_scope_forbidden`. | Security Architect | Open — mitigation is drafted in the RFC, unverified until a host implements it |
| R3 | The existing `byok-auth-modes.test.ts` conformance scenario's hard-coded enum assertion breaks (or worse, is silently left stale, undercounting the true enum) if the schema change ships without a companion conformance-suite update in the same release. | M | L | Low-Med | Explicitly named in the RFC's Conformance section and as a distinct Acceptance-criteria checklist item, rather than left implicit as "add a new scenario." | Conformance Architect | Open — process risk, mitigated by explicit checklist item |
| R4 | A client or implementer conflates `subscription` with `oauth-pkce`/`oauth-device` and applies the wrong custody/sharing assumptions (e.g., treats a subscription credential as tenant-shareable the way some `oauth-pkce` connector credentials are). | L | M | Low-Med | §B clauses 7–9 spell out co-occurrence and scope rules explicitly in normative prose; the mode table in §A gives `subscription` its own row with distinct "Credential supply" language rather than folding it into the `oauth-*` rows. | Spec Architect | Open — mitigated by spec clarity, not independently testable beyond the scope-scenario in R2 |
| R5 | If Unresolved question 2 resolves toward the official-client subprocess-harness shape (§C shape 2), the eventual implementation work is materially larger than a simple BYOK auth-mode integration (sandboxed subprocess lifecycle, session/PTY management, akin to RFC 0035) — a future implementer could underestimate scope by reading only this RFC's wire-label surface. | M | M | Medium | Implementation notes section explicitly flags the effort asymmetry and recommends a follow-up RFC extending RFC 0035 rather than treating this as a small `ctx.callAI` change. | Spec Architect | Open — informational, mitigated by explicit documentation |
