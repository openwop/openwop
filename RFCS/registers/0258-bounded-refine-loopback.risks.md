# RFC 0258 — Risk register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Target | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A host derives the visit or the re-arm from in-memory state rather than the log, and a fork cut mid-loop resumes with the gate completed: downstream runs on round-1 data, or a side-effect node fires on a refine. | M | H | High | §C.1 makes the gate's `node.completed.loopback` the only record of the decision; §C.6 binds forks to it; the `replay-rearms` leg. | Conformance Architect | at `Active` | open |
| R2 | Per-visit keys collide on hosts whose suspend nodes supply their own resume key (openwop-app's `core.chat.approvalGate` uses its node id), so visit 1 is handed visit 0's `refine` and the loop drives itself to the cap. | M | H | High | interrupt.md already requires distinct keys per visit; the `refine-rearms` leg asserts a new key; implementation note 1 names the defect. | Reference host | before advertising | open |
| R3 | History text reaches a model prompt, and a host adds run-scoped bytes (timestamps, run ids) to it, so a replayed model call's input digest diverges. | M | M | Med | §C.4 forbids them; the leg asserts the entry's exact keys. | Security Architect | at `Active` | open |
| R4 | Reviewer feedback is untrusted user text that reaches a model in the target. | H | M | Med | Unchanged from today (the feedback already reaches models through fresh runs). `SECURITY/threat-model-prompt-injection.md` applies; hosts fence it as any user input. No new invariant. | Security Architect | at `Active` | accepted |
| R5 | A client submits a `loopback` definition to a host that runs plain cycles and does not validate the closed edge schema; the edge runs unbounded to `recursionLimit`. | L | M | Low | §B binds non-advertisers to refuse; §E binds clients not to submit. | Compatibility Architect | at `Active` | open |
| R6 | A gate with a loopback offers no refine on its exhausted visit, and a card that does not read `ApprovalData.loopback` shows a missing button with no explanation. | M | L | Low | `ApprovalData.loopback.exhausted` is required on every visit; non-normative UI guidance in the PR. | Spec Architect | at `Active` | open |
