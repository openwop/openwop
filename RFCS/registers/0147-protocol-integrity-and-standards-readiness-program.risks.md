# RFC 0147 — Risk Register

Working document (not normative). Likelihood and impact use H/M/L. High/Critical risks require a named owner and target date before RFC 0147 may become `Active`.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Target / Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Existing certification bundles materially overstate behavior because required paths returned early. | H | H | **Critical** | Ship SR-1 first; inventory, invalidate, and reissue affected bundles; publish exact scope. | Conformance Architect | Target: before next certification publication; Open |
| R2 | Correcting activity identity or replay digests silently changes interpretation of stored histories. | H | H | **Critical** | Version recipes, pin per run/event, dual-read only where proven safe, migration tooling and adversarial vectors. | Compatibility Architect | Target: SR-3 `Active`; Open |
| R3 | Split-brain hosts duplicate payments/messages before a losing run is cancelled. | M | H | **Critical** | Fencing/effect ownership plus effect-level idempotency; partition tests; add compensation as defense, not primary prevention. | Security Architect | Target: SR-3 `Accepted`; Open |
| R4 | The remediation program expands the corpus further without improving implementability. | H | M | **High** | Freeze optional growth, enforce child decomposition, stable-core manifest, extension budget, independent evidence. | Lead Maintainer | Target: RFC 0147 `Active`; Open |
| R5 | A single umbrella RFC obscures incompatible compatibility classes. | M | H | **High** | No wire change lands directly from 0147; every workstream requires a child RFC and separate classification. | Compatibility Architect | Target: each child intake; Mitigated by design |
| R6 | The OpenAPI correction breaks a client that worked around `/v1/v1`. | M | M | **Medium** | Survey generated clients, document canonical endpoint, temporarily detect/warn on legacy URL in reference hosts if necessary. | SDK Maintainer | Target: SR-2 `Accepted`; Open |
| R7 | Tightening discovery schema closure breaks legal v1 extensions. | M | H | **High** | Keep runtime server-emitted shapes open in v1; catch canonical typos in authoring/certification lint; reserve strict closure for v2. | Schema Architect | Target: SR-2 `Active`; Open |
| R8 | Compensation is marketed as rollback or atomicity. | M | H | **High** | Normatively call it best-effort durable compensation; expose partial/manual states; add claims test. | Spec Architect | Target: SR-4 `Active`; Open |
| R9 | A compensating action causes a second harmful effect or leaks authority. | M | H | **Critical** | Separate idempotency, delegated identity, least privilege, approvals, egress policy, audit, and DLQ; external audit review. | Security Architect | Target: SR-4 `Accepted`; Open |
| R10 | A2A/MCP upstream protocols change again during implementation. | M | M | **Medium** | Explicit versioned profiles, compatibility matrix, upstream-version pins, scheduled refresh SLA, no generic support flag. | Interop Maintainer | Target: SR-5/SR-6 `Active`; Open |
| R11 | Dual legacy/current interop profiles create downgrade attacks. | M | H | **High** | Authenticated negotiation, minimum-version policy, audit downgrade, fail closed when policy forbids legacy. | Security Architect | Target: SR-5/SR-6 `Accepted`; Open |
| R12 | Workload provenance is mistaken for authorization and creates a confused deputy. | M | H | **Critical** | State identity ≠ authorization; re-evaluate tenant/audience/policy at every boundary; add negative delegated-chain tests. | Security Architect | Target: SR-7 `Accepted`; Open |
| R13 | Experimental OTel GenAI conventions churn and destabilize the core taxonomy. | M | M | **Medium** | Keep stable `openwop.*` canonical; make external mappings versioned and optional. | Observability Architect | Target: SR-7 `Active`; Open |
| R14 | Governance requirements cannot be satisfied because no independent maintainers volunteer. | H | H | **Critical** | Do not waive the gate; pause standards claims and optional RFC growth; fund outreach and publish the capacity constraint. | Lead Maintainer | Target: before RFC 0147 `Accepted`; Open |
| R15 | Security-audit funding or vendor availability delays the program. | M | H | **High** | Set budget/authority now, solicit multiple vendors, stage design review then implementation retest, keep claims qualified. | Lead Maintainer | Target: contract before SR-3 `Accepted`; Open |
| R16 | A sponsored Tier-3 host is perceived as steward-controlled. | M | M | **Medium** | Independent repository, maintainers, release control, deployment, and public evidence; disclose funding/support. | Governance Maintainer | Target: first Tier-3 result; Open |
| R17 | Retrospective review reopens many accepted RFCs and destabilizes v1. | M | H | **High** | Risk-rank cohorts; corrections use safety-fix/additive rules; non-safety breaks queue for v2; publish decision log. | Governance Maintainer | Target: SR-9 `Accepted`; Open |
| R18 | Publicly documenting critical defects creates exploitation risk before fixes ship. | M | H | **High** | Triage through GitHub Security Advisories; embargo exploit detail when CVE-class; keep public RFC at design/mitigation level. | Security Maintainer | Target: immediate triage; Open |
| R19 | “A-grade” becomes a one-time badge rather than a maintained evidence state. | H | M | **High** | Machine-readable evidence expiry, scheduled standards refresh, annual audit/retest, current Tier-3 suite reports. | Release Maintainer | Target: Workstream 9; Open |
| R20 | Reference-host success is again mistaken for independent protocol proof. | H | M | **High** | Evidence-tier labels are mandatory; Tier-1/Tier-2 cannot satisfy Tier-3 gates. | Governance Maintainer | Target: immediate claims policy; Open |
