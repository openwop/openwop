# RFC 0089 — Risk Register

Companion to `0089-conformance-certification-bundle.md`. Likelihood × Impact (H/M/L). Critical/High require a named mitigation owner.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **False sense of certification** — a self-published bundle is read as "certified" when its `host.commit` + results are self-reported and unverified. | M | H | **High** | §B mandates a consumer RE-DERIVE profiles from the embedded `discovery.document` (don't trust `claimedProfiles`); spec language (G5) restricts authoritative bundles to independent-verifier generation; reproducibility (re-run suite vs commit) is the integrity story. | Security Architect | Open |
| R2 | **Bundle staleness** — a published bundle ages out of sync with a changing host; readers act on a stale pass list. | M | M | Med | Required `generatedAt` + `host.commit` make every bundle a dated point-in-time attestation; `certificationBundleUrl` SHOULD point at the latest; doc says bundles are snapshots, not live state. | Spec Architect | Open |
| R3 | **Scenario-ID drift across suite versions** breaks `results.passed` diffability + the floor-scenario match in §B(2). | M | M | Med | G2/G3 audit ID stability before schema finalization; pin `suite.version` in every bundle so a verifier matches floor sets to the same suite. | Conformance Architect | Open |
| R4 | **Floor-set definition gap** (G1) — without a machine-readable floor-scenario set per profile, §B(2) can't be verified, stalling `Active`. | M | M | Med | Resolve G1 in the comment window; may land a small prerequisite (floor-set manifest) first. | Spec Architect | Open |
| R5 | **Vitest-reporter coupling** — the generator parses vitest JSON; an upstream reporter change breaks `--certify`. | L | M | Low | Parse the documented `--reporter=json` shape only, not internals; a single conformance assertion round-trips a sample so a break is caught in CI. | Conformance Architect | Open |
| R6 | **Scope creep into signing/provenance** turns an additive format RFC into a security-infrastructure project, delaying the audit's ask. | M | M | Med | Keep v1 bundle unsigned-but-reproducible (Unresolved Q3 / G4); defer signing to a clearly-scoped follow-up that wraps this payload. | Spec Architect | Open |
| R7 | **INTEROP-MATRIX double-bookkeeping** — bundles + the existing prose rows drift apart. | L | M | Low | At `Accepted`, the matrix row links the generated bundle as the source of truth; prose becomes a human summary of the machine artifact. | Conformance Architect | Open |
