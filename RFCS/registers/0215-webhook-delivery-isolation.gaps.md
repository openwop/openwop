# RFC 0215 — Gap register

Open design gaps found while authoring RFC 0215 (webhook delivery isolation and unregister-stops-delivery). Rows are keyed to the RFC. Each has an owner and a resolution path.

| ID | Section | Question / Missing Input | Owner | Resolution Path | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | §Compatibility | MyndHyve's webhook delivery worker has not been measured against §A or §B. Both motivating instances are openwop-app's. | Conformance Architect | `externally-gated:myndhyve-webhook-isolation-cut` — the first MyndHyve bundle cut on the suite minor that ships the two scenarios answers it. No steward work is owed before the scenarios exist. | The `Active → Accepted` flip (two hosts). |
| G2 | §A scope | §A governs whether an attempt *starts* behind another subscription. It does not bound how long after an event becomes due its first attempt must start. openwop-app's WHD-1 retries were each due seconds later and not claimed for minutes. That is isolation when the cause is other subscribers, and a latency SLO when it is not. | Spec Architect | `transferred:rfc-0215` — Unresolved question 4. Alternative 2 argues a latency bound is the wrong property to legislate; if one is ever wanted, it needs its own RFC with a load model. | Nothing in this RFC. |
| G3 | §Proposed invariants | No `SECURITY/threat-model-*.md` covers availability or noisy-neighbour abuse. `webhook-delivery-isolation` has no threat model to cite, and `secret-leakage` does not fit a delay. | Security Architect | `transferred:SECURITY/invariants.yaml` — resolved when the row is filed at `Active`, either by citing a new availability section in an existing threat model (interop or secret-leakage) or by filing the invariant with `threat_model:` naming the gap. | The invariant row's `threat_model` field at `Active`. |
| G4 | §A.3 / Falsifiability | The cross-tenant leg needs tenant A to hold at least the §A.2 floor of attempts open while tenant B's delivery is observed. Its pass condition is a SHOULD, so it reports. Whether a reporting leg belongs in the certified bundle, or only in the run log, is not decided. | Conformance Architect | `transferred:conformance/src/scenarios` — decided when `v2-webhook-delivery-isolation.test.ts` is written. RFC 0213's `partial-witness` disposition is the likely precedent. | Nothing before the scenario is written. |
| G5 | §B | Whether `GET /webhooks/{webhookId}/dead-letters` (RFC 0188) still answers after that subscription is unregistered, and whether existing dead-letter records outlive the unregister, is unstated. RFC 0188 requires `404 not_found` only when the facet is not advertised. | Spec Architect | `transferred:RFCS/0188-webhook-dead-letter-read.md` — a correction to RFC 0188 §A, not this RFC. §B deliberately writes no dead-letter records at unregister, so this RFC does not depend on the answer. | Nothing in this RFC. |
| G6 | Unresolved question 1 | The floor of 8 is chosen to exceed the one measured claim batch (openwop-app, 5), not derived from prior art. Hosted webhook services' published delivery designs were not surveyed. | Spec Architect | `transferred:rfc-0215` — the survey is owed before `Active`, and Unresolved question 1 stays open until it is done. | The `Draft → Active` flip. |
