# RFC 0215 — Gap register

Open design gaps found while authoring RFC 0215 (webhook delivery isolation and unregister-stops-delivery). Rows are keyed to the RFC. Each has an owner and a resolution path.

| ID | Section | Question / Missing Input | Owner | Resolution Path | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | §Compatibility | MyndHyve's webhook delivery worker has not been measured against §A or §B. Both motivating instances are openwop-app's. | Conformance Architect | `closed` 2026-09-26 — measured. MyndHyve's audit found §A held by design (one Cloud Task per subscription × event) and §B had three gaps (a `deleting` subscription still delivered; a stale existence check before the fetch; queued retries not cancelled), fixed in myndhyve#488 with the delivery timeout raised to 15 s. Its certified production cut on 2.40.0 (openwop#1595, revision `workflow-runtime-00771-tat`) records both rows `executed-pass`. | — |
| G2 | §A scope | §A governs whether an attempt *starts* behind another subscription. It does not bound how long after an event becomes due its first attempt must start. openwop-app's WHD-1 retries were each due seconds later and not claimed for minutes. That is isolation when the cause is other subscribers, and a latency SLO when it is not. | Spec Architect | `transferred:rfc-0215` — Unresolved question 4. Alternative 2 argues a latency bound is the wrong property to legislate; if one is ever wanted, it needs its own RFC with a load model. | Nothing in this RFC. |
| G3 | §Proposed invariants | No `SECURITY/threat-model-*.md` covers availability or noisy-neighbour abuse. `webhook-delivery-isolation` has no threat model to cite, and `secret-leakage` does not fit a delay. | Security Architect | `closed` 2026-09-25 — `SECURITY/threat-model-secret-leakage.md` §4.12 ("Starvation by registration, and delivery after withdrawal"), beside §4.11's registration-as-abuse analysis. It covers both rows, and the RFC's invariant table now cites it. The rows themselves are still filed at `Active`. | — |
| G4 | §A.3 / Falsifiability | The cross-tenant leg needs tenant A to hold at least the §A.2 floor of attempts open while tenant B's delivery is observed. Its pass condition is a SHOULD, so it reports. Whether a reporting leg belongs in the certified bundle, or only in the run log, is not decided. | Conformance Architect | `closed` 2026-09-25 — decided by the architecture review at `Active`: no row. A SHOULD cannot fail a certification row, and a row that only reports would sit in every bundle as `executed-pass` about a property nobody checked. The RFC's falsifiability table declares §A.3 unwitnessed. A future RFC that makes the fair share a MUST, or gives it a floor, mints the row. | — |
| G5 | §B | Whether `GET /webhooks/{webhookId}/dead-letters` (RFC 0188) still answers after that subscription is unregistered, and whether existing dead-letter records outlive the unregister, is unstated. RFC 0188 requires `404 not_found` only when the facet is not advertised. | Spec Architect | `transferred:RFCS/0188-webhook-dead-letter-read.md` — a correction to RFC 0188 §A, not this RFC. §B deliberately writes no dead-letter records at unregister, so this RFC does not depend on the answer. | Nothing in this RFC. |
| G6 | Unresolved question 1 | The floor of 8 is chosen to exceed the one measured claim batch (openwop-app, 5), not derived from prior art. Hosted webhook services' published delivery designs were not surveyed. | Spec Architect | `closed` 2026-09-25 — RFC 0215 §Prior art (Standard Webhooks, Stripe, Svix, Convoy, Hookdeck, Shopify). No surveyed system publishes a number: isolation is described as a mechanism, which meets any floor. 8 stays a measured testability floor, open to comment until `Active` (Unresolved question 1). | — |
